Ebrahim Rasromani

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at the NYU Center for Data Science, advised by Yiqiu Shen and Carlos Fernandez-Granda. My current research interests center around multimodal learning and its application in cancer screening and early detection.

Prior to starting the Ph.D., I led product development as the CTO and co-founder of Tethys AI, a healthcare technology startup. I also hold an M.S. in Data Science from NYU and a B.S. in Engineering Physics from Cornell University.

Outside of research, I enjoy sailing and scuba diving.
